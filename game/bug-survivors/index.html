Bug Survivors

Bug Survivors is a free arcade survival game from PostHog that runs in your browser. You play a hedgehog in an arena while bugs pour in from every side. You only move: your weapons fire on their own, and each one is a PostHog product, like Product Analytics, Session Replay, Feature Flags and Experiments.

Squash bugs to collect XP gems, pick a new weapon or upgrade each time you level up, and survive until the Legacy Monolith boss shows up at 3:30. Beat it and you can cash out your gold or keep climbing the wave ladder against tougher versions of the boss.

How to play

  • Move with WASD or the arrow keys, or drag on a touch screen.
  • Pick level-up cards with 1, 2 and 3. Reroll with R, skip with X, banish with B, pause with Esc.
  • Combine a maxed weapon, its partner passive and a chest to evolve it.
